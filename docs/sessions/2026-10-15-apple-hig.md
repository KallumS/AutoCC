# 2026-10-15 - The rest of Apple's HIG Foundations (1.1)

Part of the same sweep as the day before's writing change: every window in the
family brought to Apple's HIG Foundations, to the standard Transient Shuffler
already meets. Decision: [`docs/decisions/0001`](../decisions/0001-apples-hig-foundations.md);
page-by-page review: [`docs/apple-hig-review.md`](../apple-hig-review.md).
The session was interrupted once by a usage limit and picked up from a clean
tree; nothing had been committed before the break.

## What changed, in a musician's terms

- **Easier to read.** Every word stands out from what it sits on by at least
  7 to 1. The labels that were mid-grey (or, for "Fixed", the canvas hint and
  the help footer, nearly invisible) are a lighter grey; "Bypassed" is a light
  red you can read. The band names on the canvas sit on little dark plates so
  the yellow fill never washes them out.
- **Bigger if you want it.** *Options → Interface Size* - 100, 125, 150 or
  200% - saved with the project. Everything grows together, rows wrap, and the
  window scrolls if it is still too small. Full resolution on a Retina screen,
  and the system font (San Francisco / Segoe UI).
- **Comfortable targets.** Buttons 28 points tall, 12 apart, never narrower
  than their words; a lane's on/off box can be hit anywhere in a 28-point
  square.
- **The keyboard alone.** Tab / Shift-Tab, a white ring, Return or Space to
  press, arrows to change a value (Shift finer), Esc to let go.
- **Not colour alone.** The chosen preset has a dark bar under its name as
  well as the yellow.
- **Messages at the top, and they stay.** The help footer is gone; a bar at
  the top says what the control under the pointer does, what an action just
  did (Copy Shape to All, Clear Lane, Reset Curves, Panic), or that the engine
  is bypassed. A message stays until *Dismiss*.
- The big yellow "AutoCC" title and the tagline went (REAPER's title bar has
  the name); the tagline is the bar's resting text.

Version 1.0 → 1.1 in the script header. Saved projects and automation are
untouched: no slider changed; the interface size is a new last field in
`@serialize`, read only when it is there.

## How it was checked

There was no harness. `tools/` now has one, built on ysfx (the same EEL2 and
LICE as REAPER), after Instrument Creator's and Reverberator's ysfx hosts:

- `tools/build_host.sh` builds `tools/build/uihost` (git-ignored) from ysfx at
  a pinned commit; it was run from scratch into an empty folder to prove it.
- `tools/uicheck.py` instruments a temporary copy (each `gfx_drawstr` and
  `gfx_rect` in `@gfx` logged; the pixels under each string read with
  `gfx_getpixel` before it is drawn) and drives it: 100 checks, all passing.
- `tools/bite.py` breaks the file in a temporary copy and reruns the checker.

**Sabotages, 30, every one caught (BIT)** after one gap was closed:
secondary text back to #8A919C; "Bypassed" in the house red; the selected
row's words left grey (6.6:1 on the lighter row); band names without plates;
buttons not registering for Tab; Peak registered before Floor; the ring in the
accent (twice - once through the ramp check, once cleanly through the ring
check alone); no ring; Return/Space dead; Shift not finer; Esc keeping focus;
Shift+Tab going forward; no ink bar; controls 26 tall; buttons 6 apart; a
button narrower than its label; 11-point labels; field height not scaled;
a message on a one-second timer; the help back in a footer; the interface
size not saved; the menu's 200% item mis-wired; no Retina; a neutral grey;
`want_all_kb` set; a disabled Hold still in the Tab order; Dismiss doing
nothing; the focus not scrolled down into view; the focus not scrolled up.

**The one MISSED, now closed:** "focus not scrolled into view" first broke only
the scrolling *up* branch, and the test only scrolled *down* (Shift+Tab from
the top to Engine). A second check now Tabs round to Default and asks that it
is back in view; both directions bite.

Screenshots were looked at: 100%, the busy state (user lane, One-Shot,
bypassed, a note sounding, a message), the focus ring on a field with the
playhead running, 200% in a small window (wrapped presets, ellipsis in the
bar) and 200% in a window twice the size. Lines looked hair-thin at 200%, so
curves and ticks now thicken with the size (`tline`).

## Things that looked wrong and were the rig

- **ysfx packs arrow keys backwards** (`'up'` arrives as 0x7075; REAPER
  documents 30064) and **reports Shift only during a click**. Every arrow and
  Shift+Tab test failed at first for that reason, not the plug-in's. The
  checker now hands keys in through `t_getchar()` in its instrumented copy, in
  REAPER's documented form.
- Contrast "failures" of 1.17:1 at large sizes were strings scrolled off the
  bottom of the window, sampled against nothing; the check now skips text not
  on screen.
- "The engine is bypassed" was not found because the log kept only 20
  characters of each string; it keeps 60 now.
- The "every control doubles at 200%" check needed a little slack (3%):
  measured text is not exactly twice as wide at twice the size.

## Found on the way

- `docs/palette-preview.svg` was not well-formed XML: a caption contained a
  bare `<` ("R < G < B"), so a strict viewer would refuse it. Escaped. Its
  text colours were lifted to #B0B6C0 by hand, since no generator for it
  exists in the repository (CLAUDE.md says to regenerate it; there is nothing
  to regenerate it with). It still shows the 1.0 layout.
